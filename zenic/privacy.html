Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

The short version: Zenic does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

1. Data you enter

Zenic lets you enter information such as your first name (optional), the nicotine product you use, how much you use and spend, your quit date, your reasons for quitting, cravings, mood check-ins and personal notes. This information is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage and an App Group container shared with Zenic's widgets. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

If you use iCloud or encrypted device backups, Apple may include this data in your backups according to your device settings. Apple's handling of backups is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

2. Data we collect

None. Zenic does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services or any other third-party code that collects data. We do not use cookies or device identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Zenic checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. Apple may share anonymized, aggregate sales reports with us (for example, the number of subscriptions sold per country), which do not identify you.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Zenic schedules reminders locally on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

5. On-device AI coach

On supported devices, Zenic's coach uses Apple's on-device language model. Your messages are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us. Conversations are not stored after you leave the coach screen. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the coach uses built-in guidance that works fully offline.

6. Health information

Zenic provides general wellness information to support quitting nicotine. It is not a medical device and does not read data from Apple Health. The information you enter about your habits never leaves your device.

7. Children

Zenic is not directed at children under 13, and because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your control

You can edit your plan at any time in Settings, and delete all of your data using Settings → Reset all data or by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Visit the support page.
