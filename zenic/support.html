Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Zenic → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage subscription inside Zenic's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

I reinstalled or got a new iPhone

Open Zenic and tap Restore on the welcome screen or paywall (or Settings → Restore purchases). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the Zenic purchase.

Using Zenic

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Zenic and choose the streak or quick-log widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen and add Zenic.

I slipped. Do I lose my progress?

No. Tap I slipped on the Home tab and choose whether to keep your streak or restart the timer. Your money saved and past streaks are always kept.

Can I switch from cold turkey to a taper plan?

Yes — go to Settings → Edit my plan.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Zenic has no accounts and no servers. See our privacy policy.

Health

Zenic offers general wellness support and is not medical advice. If withdrawal symptoms feel severe, talk to a doctor, pharmacist or a local quitline. In the US you can call 1-800-QUIT-NOW or text DITCHVAPE to 88709.
