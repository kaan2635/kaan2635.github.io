How Zenic uses them

  • Heart rate & blood pressure — nicotine is a stimulant; effects begin to fade within about 20 minutes.
  • Nicotine half-life — roughly two hours, so levels drop by over 90% within about eight hours.
  • Withdrawal — typically peaks in the first few days and eases over 2–4 weeks.
  • Brain receptors — return toward non-user levels within about three months.
  • Mental health — quitting is associated with lower anxiety, depression and stress.
